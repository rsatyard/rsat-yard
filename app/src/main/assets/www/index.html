Scan

Point the camera at the number painted on the tank (4 letters + 7 digits), then tap OK or Edit to correct it. Or type a slot (A1) or tank number in the box.

Yard map

Loaded Empty clean Empty dirty Gas tank laden Gas tank empty Free space

Each slot holds up to 5 tanks (4 in A11–C11 and M15–O15). Each dot is one space, colored by the tank in it; a dark border means full. Tap a slot to see or update its tanks. Swipe sideways to see the whole yard.

Tank register

Export to Excel

Choose what goes into the file. Each row is one visit of a tank to the yard, from date in to date out. Your choices are remembered on this device.

Columns in the file

Print slot labels

A PDF with 10 labels per A4 page. Cut along the dashed lines and stick each label on its slot.

Accounts

Only approved accounts can see the yard. Staff can record and change tanks; admins can also manage accounts here.

With a password, the account is ready to sign in. Without one, the person taps Create account with this email and gets in straight away.

Oldest app version allowed

Older phone apps keep working after an update. Only when an update must reach everyone (for example a security fix), raise this number: phones on an older version are then asked to update before they can continue. 0 allows every version. The website is always up to date.
